Model Tone Index (MTI)

MTI Profiler

A Universal Standard for AI Behavior.

The AI industry faces a fragmentation problem. Terms like "creative" or "analytical" are subjective, making it difficult to specify, compare, and reproduce AI behavior. The Model Tone Index (MTI) solves this by providing a standardized, measurable system for the "tone" of an AI model.

For a developer, a hex code like #38BDF8CC is more than a color; it's a compact, machine-readable data packet that specifies four distinct variables in a single, universally understood format. It's a spec, not a suggestion.

The MTI Data Model:

  • Hue (0-360): Defines the AI's core function, or Archetype.
  • Saturation (0-100): Sets the intensity of its personality, or Persona.
  • Lightness (0-100): Controls its adherence to facts, or Rigor.
  • Alpha (0-100): Determines the level of detail, or Verbosity.
